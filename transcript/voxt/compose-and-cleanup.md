## Prose · Where this chapter's claims come from

Step 1 maps one recognized result to three task routes and distinguishes content from a Rewrite instruction. Step 2 orders system, optional history and user messages, separating stable instructions from this run's input. Step 3 simplifies a Transcribe cleanup prompt into four instruction layers and one input; its deep dive covers omitted layers and the custom-prompt branch. Step 4 compares the three answer contracts, including built-in `<output>` extraction and first-write JSON fallback. Step 5 splits a constructed long Transcribe input into three requests and joins their answers in speech order; its deep dive covers thresholds, concurrency and draft publication. Step 6 fails one constructed piece, keeps that piece's recognized text and ends with degraded `Done + ProblemLLM`; its deep dive covers skip, deadlines and late answers. Step 7 compares one strict selected-Translate echo retry with Rewrite's lack of a spoken-instruction fallback, including their `Failed` exits. Step 8 forces a constructed length-limited Translate answer into one answer card with degraded `Done + ProblemTruncated`, never an unseen insert. The inputs and answers are constructed teaching examples, not measured model traffic.
