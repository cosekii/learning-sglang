## Prose · Where this chapter's claims come from

Step 1 reads the two-pass shape. While a meeting records, each track runs live recognition (`Voxt/internal/meeting/track_transcribe.go:52-72`, `101-113`); after it ends, `FinalizePass` re-reads the archived WAVs and replaces the live segments (`Voxt/internal/meeting/finalize.go:28-46`). The two routes are Chapter 4's streaming and file routes.

Step 2 reads the live commit. A `Committer` turns streaming partials into sentence commits: a phrase commits at 750 ms of quiet (300 ms once it passes 4 s), and one live segment never exceeds 8 s (`Voxt/internal/meeting/committer.go:10-15`, `40-66`). A stream also rotates at 10 minutes of age or after 30 s without partials (`Voxt/internal/meeting/live.go:12-13`, `105`, `194-206`); rotation is a reliability detail this step only footnotes.

Step 3 reads the per-track pass. `FinalizePass` walks the mode's sources one at a time, reports progress as `done tracks ÷ track count`, and each track reads its WAV, finds speech, plans chunks and transcribes them in file mode (`Voxt/internal/meeting/finalize.go:47-76`, `113-168`). A track with no file or no audio has nothing to replace; a track with audio but no words replaces with an empty set (`finalize.go:115-131`, `158`, `168`).

Step 4 reads the replace. `ReplaceSegments` deletes that track's stored lines and inserts the new ones in one transaction (`Voxt/internal/store/sqlitestore/meetings.go:165-194`). The example's four live lines against three final lines are constructed: the live pass commits at your pauses (Step 2), the file read hears whole regions.

Step 5 reads the failure path. A track whose re-read fails is skipped and keeps its live segments; the first error marks the meeting interrupted and, with keep-audio off, keeps the recording seven days for a reprocess (`finalize.go:60-67`, `92-104`, `467-478`; `ErrFinalizeRetry` at `finalize.go:24-25`). `Reprocess` reruns the pass, and a done meeting that kept no recording returns `ErrNoRecording` — its transcript stays as it is (`Voxt/internal/meeting/manager_finalize.go:11-26`).

Step 6 reads the echo check. Speech from the speakers reaches the microphone (#3982): a mic line whose words share 3-word runs with the system speech within ±2 s, over at least half the line, is an echo and is dropped whole, never rewritten (`Voxt/internal/meeting/echo.go:15-46`, `96-102`). The check runs twice: live, a mic echo is never committed (`track_transcribe.go:235-241`), and after the final pass, on both final texts (`finalize.go:77-81`).

Constructed parts: the timestamps and wordings of the example lines (`hi`, `everyone`, `we should ship Friday`, …) are invented. No real meeting, recognition model or diarizer was run. How the system track's final lines get Speaker 1 / Speaker 2 is Chapter 14.
