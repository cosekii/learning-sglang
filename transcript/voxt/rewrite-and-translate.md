## Prose · Where this chapter's claims come from

Step 1 draws the two routes. Rewrite and Translate are session kinds of their own (`Voxt/internal/session/write.go:35`, `write.go:46`), and the card the Rewrite shortcut opens is documented at the top of `Voxt/rewrite_box.go:3-15`: the shortcut starts the microphone and opens the card together, the selected text shows in it as the input, and the answer goes back into the app the user was in. Both features compose their request through the same language-model connection Chapter 5 draws (`Voxt/internal/compose/compose.go`).

Step 2 reads the selection. `readSelection` reads the selected text of the app in front and returns "" for none or when it could not be read (`Voxt/internal/session/run_selection.go:32-104`). The budgets on the stage are the code's: a caret-only reading waits out a 600 ms settle, polling every 60 ms, because a selection made with ⌘A in the shortcut's motion can lag (`run_selection.go:15-27`); Rewrite's own copy budget is 1,500 ms (`Voxt/internal/session/session.go:366-370`), and while the shortcut's keys stay down the read retries every 100 ms, the last time once the recording ends (`run_selection.go:47-66`, `run_selection.go:107-109`). The helper side of the read — an all-whitespace or U+FFFC-only reading counts as no reading, the ⌘C waits up to 800 ms for held modifiers, snapshots the clipboard, waits up to 300 ms for a change and puts the snapshot back — is the documented protocol (`Voxt/docs/helper-protocol.md:125`) and its implementation (`Voxt/helper/Sources/VoxtNewHelperKit/SelectionRead.swift:15-19`).

Step 3 opens the card. The card holds the selection as one block, the typed text, and up to 4 pictures (`boxMaxImages`, `Voxt/rewrite_box.go:49-51`); the shortcut again or Enter sends, Esc closes without sending (`rewrite_box.go:3-15`). A send that fails brings the card back with the error and everything kept, and Enter sends the same instruction again (`rewrite_box.go:28-30`).

Step 4 assembles the request. Rewrite's compose input keeps a fixed order — the source, the typed text, the spoken instruction — and typed text alone takes the source's place (`write.go:46-57`). The user message wraps the parts in that order: `<selected_text>`, `<typed_text>`, `<instruction>` (`Voxt/internal/compose/compose.go:419-436`). With nothing selected or typed, the request is the speech alone (`rewrite_box.go:12-15`). The box's latest text and pictures are read when the answer is asked for and win over the clipboard's picture and the window's; a window that will not be sent is never shot (`write.go:71-140`).

Step 5 brings the answer back. A streaming answer is published at most every 40 ms (`write.go:17-18`). Writing from scratch asks for a JSON answer `{title, content}` (`write.go:20-25`, `write.go:53-56`); a follow-up answers in plain text and replays the earlier turns as the conversation's history (`compose.go:395-400`). Before inserting, the deliver checks run with Replace, and a refused insert keeps the answer in the card (`write.go:199-208`); an insert activates the original app first (`rewrite_box.go:17-25`). Over another app's full screen the card is a panel, because macOS shows no ordinary window there (`rewrite_box.go:22-25`). A failed request ends in the card, to try again (`write.go:62-66`; the card's buttons, `Voxt/session_answer.go:3-5`).

Step 6 is Translate on the same route. The compose input carries the target language, and with no language picked for this text the answer comes back in the user's main language (`write.go:35-44`). A selected text translates directly, not as speech (`compose.go:139-141`). An answer that looks untranslated — the source again, the whole source beside its translation, or mostly outside the target's script, with 〈指令〉 tags ignored — is asked again with the Strict layer, and a failed retry keeps the first answer (`Voxt/internal/compose/translate.go:40-75`, `write.go:215-231`).

Constructed parts: the sentence "i might be able to come friday, not sure yet", the typed line, the instruction, the rewritten answer and the two translation examples are invented; every budget, limit, order and fallback is the code's. No app, helper or language model was run.
