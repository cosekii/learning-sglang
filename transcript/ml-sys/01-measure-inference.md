## Prose · Where this chapter's claims come from

The 250 ms value is a constructed example from [zero-to-sglang Part I Chapter 5: Introduction to Benchmark](https://zero-to-sglang.datawhale.cc/eng/part1/Chapter5_Introduction_to_Benchmark.html), §3.1. The later token times are constructed additions to that example, not measurements from this computer. The TTFT and ITL definitions follow the same section and *Inference Engineering* §1.4: TTFT runs from sending a request until its first output token arrives, and ITL is the gap between neighboring output tokens. TPOT follows the course's post-first-token boundary: divide the ITL total by the number of gaps, not by all output tokens. E2E latency follows the course boundary from request sent to completion: subtract the endpoints, or add TTFT to all post-first-token gaps.
