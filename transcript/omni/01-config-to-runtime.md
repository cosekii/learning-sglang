## Prose · Where this chapter's claims come from

The five fractions come from `examples/configs/qwen3_omni_colocated_h100_bf16.yaml` in SGLang-Omni. The model's colocated placement puts these five GPU stages on one card; `preprocessing` and `decode` are separate CPU stages and do not appear in this GPU budget. The 6% is the unassigned remainder, not a separate reserve field and not a measurement of live memory use.
