<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

Step 1 uses a constructed 80-GiB card, not a memory measurement. It places 60 GiB of weights on the card, leaving 20 GiB for the KV budget. The two fractions are 60 ÷ 80 = 75% and 20 ÷ 80 = 25%, so they account for the whole constructed card. This example isolates the arithmetic; it does not claim that a real model has these sizes or that a live free-memory reading is a KV allocation.

Step 2 uses the paired unit-test cases in `test_placement.py`, not B200 measurements. The stage first converts each fraction into bytes, then adds the reserve, forms the combined declaration and compares it with the physical limit. On a teaching 80-GiB card, a 0.50 fraction plus a 20-GiB total reserve declares 60 GiB and passes. A 0.90 fraction plus the same reserve declares 92 GiB and fails by 12 GiB. The reserve denotes the stage footprint, including its two-GiB KV pool, so that pool is not added to the same reserve again. Placement separately checks that summed KV pools alone fit.

Step 3 compares three independent cases, each with its own two-GiB `engine.kv_cache_bytes` declaration. The stage plays the first claim, the empty-case cleanup check and the second claim before showing the outcomes. The stage factory must construct exactly one SGLang engine that consumes the declaration. Zero consumers leave it unclaimed and raise during cleanup; a second consumer raises instead of sizing two full pools from one declaration. The two-GiB value is illustrative.

Step 4 keeps `weight_share=off` as a two-copy baseline and plays the shipped MOSS same-GPU DP2 example on the right. Replica 0 loads one symbolic W as leader and publishes CUDA IPC handles that let the follower open that GPU storage. Replica 1 then aliases the same W instead of copying it. Only after that attachment do two orange KV pools appear, one per replica. The shipped config declares `max_total_tokens=30000` instead of Step 3's `engine.kv_cache_bytes`; the two settings are mutually exclusive. A separate final phase counts one W and adds the two caps to 60,000 tokens. For scale only, 30,000 equals 30 × 1,000 tokens; this does not promise 30 concurrent prompts. Graphs and request state also stay private. The W boxes are symbolic and make no byte-saving claim.

Step 5 constructs two eligible, single-GPU, non-TP processes, one TP process, and one process spanning two physical GPUs. The stage excludes TP, resolves physical GPU IDs, and rejects the multi-GPU process before any lease. Both eligible processes land on one physical card labeled GPU-A, where the `2 ≥ 2` check meets this `mps=auto` example's minimum. The pipeline then acquires one MPS lease for that card before either client joins. The stage reveals unique name tags `t0` and `t1` with the joining arrows, then matches them to process 0 and process 1 during verification. The code calls these tags per-process tokens. The lease is this pipeline's key for MPS checks and cleanup, not ownership of the whole GPU or a memory allocation. Process 0's two-GiB teaching KV declaration stays two GiB before the lease and after verification.

Step 6 follows AuK serial offload through conditioning, DiT and VAE. One request owns the path, one component is resident during each transition and the other two sleep. The old component sleeps before the next wakes. Ownership ends only after terminal-result delivery, failure or cancellation; another request can then begin. Offload requires one CUDA GPU, one process replica, `tp_size=1` and batch size one. Component sizes differ, so two sleeping boxes do not prove a two-thirds byte saving.

Source revision for the chapter: `sglang-omni origin/main@db7cbf39ccffde7f95b81c2dcc7da1b52a5c0bb6`. The deep dive lists the exact files and lines.

<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

第 1 步使用构造的 80 GiB 卡，不是显存实测。先放进 60 GiB 权重，剩下 20 GiB 作为 KV 预算。两项比例分别是 60 ÷ 80 = 75% 和 20 ÷ 80 = 25%，合起来正好是这张构造卡。这个例子只展示算术，不声称真实模型具有这些大小，也不把实时空闲显存读数当成已经分配的 KV。

第 2 步使用 `test_placement.py` 的一对单元测试，不是 B200 实测。舞台先把两个比例换成字节，再加上 reserve、得到合并申报量，最后与物理上限比较。在教学用 80 GiB 卡上，0.50 比例加 20 GiB 总 reserve 申报 60 GiB，可以通过。0.90 比例加同一 reserve 申报 92 GiB，超出 12 GiB。reserve 表示 stage 整体占用，包括其中 2 GiB KV 池，所以不能再把这个池加到同一份 reserve 上。placement 还单独检查所有 KV 池之和能否装下。

第 3 步比较三种独立情况，每幅都有自己的 2 GiB `engine.kv_cache_bytes` 申报。舞台先播放第一次领取，再检查没有 engine 时的清理和第二次领取，最后显示结果。stage 工厂必须构造恰好一个 SGLang engine 来领取它。没有 engine 领取会在清理时报错；第二个 engine 领取也会报错，不能让一份申报变成两份完整 KV 池。2 GiB 是教学值。

第 4 步左侧保留 `weight_share=off` 的两份权重对照，右侧播放 MOSS 同卡 DP2 示例。replica 0 作为 leader 加载一份符号化 W，发布 CUDA IPC handle，让 follower 能打开这份 GPU 存储。replica 1 随后指向同一个 W，不再复制。附着完成后才出现两个橙色 KV 池，每个副本一个。示例配置申报 `max_total_tokens=30000`，不用第 3 步的 `engine.kv_cache_bytes`，两种设置互斥。最后一相再数出一份 W，把两个池的上限加成 60000 token。只作大小比较，30000 等于 30 × 1000 token，不保证能同时放下 30 份 prompt。graph 和请求状态也保持私有。W 方框只是符号，不声称节省多少字节。

第 5 步构造两个合格的单卡、非 TP 进程、一个 TP 进程和一个跨两张物理卡的进程。舞台先排除 TP，再解析物理 GPU ID，并在任何租约之前拒绝跨卡进程。两个合格进程落到标为 GPU-A 的同一张物理卡，`2 ≥ 2` 达到这个 `mps=auto` 示例的启用下限。随后 pipeline 为这张卡取得一份 MPS 租约，两个 client 才开始加入。加入箭头出现时，舞台显示唯一名牌 `t0` 和 `t1`，验证时再把它们分别对到 process 0 和 process 1。代码把这些名牌叫作每进程 token。租约是这个 pipeline 做 MPS 检查和清理的钥匙，不表示独占整张 GPU，也不是显存分配。process 0 的 2 GiB 教学 KV 申报在租约前和验证后都保持 2 GiB。

第 6 步跟随 AuK serial offload 走过 conditioning、DiT 和 VAE。一个请求持有整条路线；每次换组件时一件常驻，另外两件休眠。旧组件先休眠，下一组件再唤醒。终态结果交付、失败或取消后才释放所有权，下一请求才能开始。offload 要求一张 CUDA GPU、一个进程副本、`tp_size=1` 和 batch size 1。组件大小不同，两件休眠不证明节省三分之二字节。
