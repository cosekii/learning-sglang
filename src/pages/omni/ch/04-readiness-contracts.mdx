<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

Step 1 constructs an image-only request A in the speech topology. Its expected sources are `image_encoder` and `preprocessing`. The image result arrives first and is buffered; preprocessing arrives second, resolves the expected-source set and completes the fan-in. The snapshots 0/2, 1/2 and 2/2 count arrived payloads for this teaching example, not seconds or throughput. The figure covers dispatch after a one-shot fan-in into `thinker`, not the start of prefill. Streams, per-step feedback and data release come later.

Step 2 constructs a three-chunk `thinker → talker_ar` stream. This stage accepts stream data before the whole result. The first two snapshots store one and two chunks while the whole result has not arrived. By the third snapshot, chunk 2 and the whole result have arrived. The scheduler keeps early chunks in arrival order and later attaches them to the payload. The figure covers receiving and storage, not the start of computation. Three chunks are an example, not a required stream length.

Step 3 uses the default speech configuration: early request construction is enabled with a five-usable-chunk threshold. Four usable chunks with an open stream stay deferred; five enter the request builder. A finished stream passes regardless of the count, illustrated by two usable chunks. A trailing `im_end` chunk is excluded from the usable count. Entering the builder is not admission or GPU execution. Five is the default, while the implementation's configurable floor is one.

Step 4 follows one request while its thinker text stream remains open. The code predictor runs inside `talker_ar` and produces both audio codes and a summed-embedding feedback row. Codes go to `code2wav`; feedback enters that request's feedback queue. The next decode row requires feedback plus the next thinker text. With feedback but no text, it waits unless the text stream is done and `tts_pad_embed` can fill the text role. Unready rows are excluded individually; the whole batch need not stop. The chapter stops before DataRef storage and release.

<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

第 1 步构造 speech 路线里只有图的请求 A，应等 `image_encoder` 和 `preprocessing` 两份结果。image 的完整结果先到并存下，preprocessing 随后到达，确定应等来源并补齐两路输入。0/2、1/2、2/2 数的是这个教学例子里已经到达的结果，不是等待秒数或吞吐量。本图只讲 one-shot 汇聚后交给 scheduler，不表示 prefill 已开始；流、逐步反馈和数据释放留到后面。

第 2 步构造 `thinker → talker_ar` 的流，本例有 3 个 chunk。这个 stage 允许整份结果还没到就先收流数据。前两张快照分别存下 1、2 个 chunk，整份结果还未到；第三张快照里，chunk 2 和整份结果都已到。scheduler 按到达顺序保存早到的 chunk，随后把它们附到 payload。图中只讲接收和保存，不表示开始计算。每个结果不固定为 3 个 chunk。

第 3 步使用 speech 默认配置：允许提前构建，阈值是 5 段可用 chunk。流未结束时，4 段继续等待，5 段进入请求构建器；流结束后不限制数量，图中用 2 段展示。末尾的 `im_end` chunk 不计入可用数量。进入构建器不等于准入或 GPU 执行。5 是默认配置，当前实现允许配置的硬下限是 1。

第 4 步跟踪 thinker 文字流尚未结束的同一个请求。code predictor 在 `talker_ar` 内运行，同时产出声音 codes 和 summed-embedding 反馈行。codes 发给 `code2wav`，feedback 进入这个请求的反馈队列。下一步 decode 需要 feedback 加下一段 thinker 文字。有 feedback 但没文字时，除非文字流已结束且可用 `tts_pad_embed` 补上，否则继续等待。未就绪的请求行单独排除，不要求整个 batch 停下。本章讲到就绪为止，DataRef 的存储和释放留给下一章。
