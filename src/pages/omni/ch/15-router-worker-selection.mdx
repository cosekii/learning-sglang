<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

Step 1 constructs two worker profiles from the typed-image routing case in `sglang_omni_router/tests/chat_http.rs:1075-1098`. Worker A accepts text; worker B accepts typed image input. One image request has one compatible worker, so turn order cannot replace compatibility filtering. The ordinary selection path filters profiles before health and policy selection (`sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/mod.rs:338-373,544-578`). The example does not measure latency or claim that two workers always need request-body classification.

Step 2 separates the two request paths in `sglang_omni_router/src/http_generation/mod.rs:82-153`. A direct cohort shares one concrete default model and equivalent generation profiles, proved at startup in `worker_pool/mod.rs:804-840`. It selects before streaming the request body. A heterogeneous cohort buffers and classifies the body before selection, then forwards the original bytes (`http_generation/classify.rs:48-120`). Classification does not perform full application validation or rebuild the JSON.

Step 3 follows the correlated-profile rule in `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/profile.rs:754-904`. One individual row must satisfy the complete requirement; set-valued capabilities may contain extras, but facts cannot come from different rows. The constructed A rows follow the cross-row regression test at `profile.rs:1311-1334`: model M from one row cannot combine with another row's modalities and stream mode. B has one complete matching row.

Step 4 follows `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/health.rs:10-17,231-308` and the default thresholds in `sglang_omni_router/src/config.rs:290-325`. Workers start unknown; only healthy workers are routable (`worker_pool/mod.rs:124-126`). Any 2xx probe status, meaning HTTP status 200 through 299, succeeds. Another status, transport error or timeout fails; a timeout means no reply by the probe deadline. Two consecutive successes mark healthy and three consecutive failures mark unhealthy by default. A success resets the failure streak, a failure resets the success streak, and one later result does not immediately reverse a reached state.

Step 5 follows `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/selection.rs:14-23,49-60`. The selector scans one shared recency list, picks the first eligible worker and moves that worker to the back of the entire list. With A, B and C all eligible, six selections are A, B, C, A, B and C. Ineligible workers are skipped without leaving the list, and changing candidate subsets does not reset the order. Round robin does not read active-request counts, so held leases do not change the displayed sequence (`worker_pool/mod.rs:1263-1356`).

Step 6 follows `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/mod.rs:581-605`. Least requests finds the minimum active load among eligible workers, then uses the same recency selector for workers tied at that minimum. The constructed rows start at loads 2, 0, 0 and hold each new ordinary request, producing B, C, B and C with their ties and unique minima shown. With the same initial recency `[A,B,C]` and loads `[2,0,0]`, round robin selects A while least requests selects B. Selection, load observation and the following increment share one lock, so concurrent choosers cannot reserve from the same stale counts (`selection.rs:7-12`; concurrency test at `worker_pool/mod.rs:1410-1439`). These are router-held admission credits, not GPU utilization or the worker's internal queue.

Step 7 follows `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/admission.rs:49-88` and `sglang_omni_router/src/http_relay/response_body.rs:16-101`. Selection constructs a `RequestLease`, a token that holds active +1, and increments the chosen worker before dispatch. Dispatch means sending the request to that worker. The lease moves into `DirectResponseBody` after valid response headers, so headers do not release the count. EOF, meaning the end of the response, an upstream body error, or dropping the response before completion releases the lease and its admission permits exactly once. HTTP worker load is accounting for least requests, not a per-worker admission cap. One ordinary request contributes one credit; a speech batch contributes one per item.

Step 8 starts a new example with constructed loads, not a continuation of Step 6's final 2, 2, 2 or Step 7's fresh single request. It combines the chapter's filters and least-requests counts, then follows a constructed post-commit reset. The upstream client disables retries (`sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/resolver.rs:108-125`), and `sglang_omni_router/src/http_relay/mod.rs:151-209` sends one request to the selected target with no alternate-dispatch loop. The one-worker integration test at `sglang_omni_router/tests/chat_http.rs:1419-1432` demonstrates a reset after SSE headers and partial data: the client receives `200` and `data: one` without `[DONE]`, and the router releases capacity. The no-retry conclusion comes from the disabled-retry and single-send code, not from that test. A transport or body error can request an immediate probe, but the health thresholds from Step 4 decide later eligibility. The next chapter is worker failure and graceful exit.

Code references in this chapter pin to `sglang-omni origin/main@3a31bd72e034db37ce5115a03dd4827b449683be`. Examples are constructed from source rules; the chapter does not run a router or benchmark.

<details class="i18n-zh">
<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

第 1 步根据 `sglang_omni_router/tests/chat_http.rs:1075-1098` 的 typed-image 路由用例构造两个 worker 能力。worker A 接收文字，worker B 接收 typed image 输入。一个图片请求只有一个兼容 worker，所以轮流顺序不能代替兼容性过滤。普通选择路径先过滤 profile，再检查 health 和选择策略（`sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/mod.rs:338-373,544-578`）。本例不测量延迟，也不声称两个 worker 一定需要请求体分类。

第 2 步分开 `sglang_omni_router/src/http_generation/mod.rs:82-153` 的两条请求路径。direct 组有同一个具体默认模型和等价 generation profiles，启动时由 `worker_pool/mod.rs:804-840` 验证；它先选择，再流式发送请求体。异构组先缓冲并分类请求体，再选择，随后转发原始字节（`http_generation/classify.rs:48-120`）。分类不做完整应用校验，也不重建 JSON。

第 3 步依据 `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/profile.rs:754-904` 的相关联 profile 规则。同一个独立行必须满足完整要求；集合能力可以多出一些，但不能从不同行拼事实。构造的 A 两行来自 `profile.rs:1311-1334` 的跨行回归测试：不能把一行的 model M 与另一行的模态和流式模式拼起来。B 有一行完整匹配。

第 4 步依据 `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/health.rs:10-17,231-308` 和 `sglang_omni_router/src/config.rs:290-325` 的默认门槛。worker 从 unknown 开始，只有 healthy 可路由（`worker_pool/mod.rs:124-126`）。探测状态为任意 2xx 就算成功，即 HTTP 状态 200 到 299；其他状态、传输错误或 timeout 算失败，timeout 指超过探测期限仍没有回应。默认连续 2 次成功转为 healthy，连续 3 次失败转为 unhealthy。成功会清空失败连击，失败会清空成功连击；已经达到某个状态后，单独一次相反结果不会立刻翻转它。

第 5 步依据 `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/selection.rs:14-23,49-60`。selector 扫描一个共享的最近使用顺序，取第一个候选 worker，再把它移到整个列表的末尾。A、B、C 都合格时，六次选择依次是 A、B、C、A、B、C。不合格 worker 被跳过但不移出列表，候选集合变化也不会重置顺序。round robin 不读取活跃请求数，所以凭证保持占用也不会改变图上的顺序（`worker_pool/mod.rs:1263-1356`）。

第 6 步依据 `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/mod.rs:581-605`。least requests 先找候选 worker 的最小活跃数，再用同一个最近使用顺序选择并列最少的 worker。构造行从负载 2、0、0 开始，每个新普通请求都保持占用，因此依次选 B、C、B、C，图上同时标出并列和唯一最小值。在同样的初始顺序 `[A,B,C]` 和负载 `[2,0,0]` 下，round robin 会选 A，least requests 会选 B。选择、读取负载和随后加一共享一把锁，避免并发选择者根据同一组旧计数预留（`selection.rs:7-12`；并发测试在 `worker_pool/mod.rs:1410-1439`）。这些数字是 router 持有的 admission credits，不是 GPU 利用率或 worker 内部队列。

第 7 步依据 `sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/admission.rs:49-88` 和 `sglang_omni_router/src/http_relay/response_body.rs:16-101`。选择时构造 `RequestLease`，也就是持有活跃数 +1 的凭证，并在 dispatch 前增加被选 worker 的计数。dispatch 是把请求发送给这个 worker。有效响应头到达后，凭证移入 `DirectResponseBody`，所以响应头不会释放计数。EOF 是响应结束；EOF、上游 body 错误或响应在完成前被丢弃，都会恰好收回一次凭证和它的 admission permits。HTTP worker 负载是 least requests 的计数，不是每个 worker 的 admission 上限。普通请求贡献 1 credit，speech batch 每条目贡献 1 credit。

第 8 步使用构造负载另起一例，不接着第 6 步最后的 2、2、2，也不接着第 7 步的新单个请求。它组合本章的过滤和 least-requests 计数，再跟随一个构造的提交后 reset。上游客户端禁用重试（`sglang_omni_router/src/worker_pool/resolver.rs:108-125`），`sglang_omni_router/src/http_relay/mod.rs:151-209` 只向选中目标发送一次请求，没有备选 dispatch 循环。`sglang_omni_router/tests/chat_http.rs:1419-1432` 的单 worker 集成测试展示了 SSE 响应头和部分数据之后的 reset：客户端收到 `200` 和 `data: one`，没有 `[DONE]`，router 释放容量。不重试的结论来自禁用重试和单次发送的代码，不来自这个测试。传输或 body 错误可以请求立即探测，但后续能否入选仍由第 4 步的 health 门槛决定。下一章是 worker 故障与退出。

本章代码出处固定到 `sglang-omni origin/main@3a31bd72e034db37ce5115a03dd4827b449683be`。示例按源码规则构造，本章没有运行 router 或 benchmark。
