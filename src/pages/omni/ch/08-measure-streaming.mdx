<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

Step 1 repeats the PR #1235 measurements already shown in Learning SGLang Ch. 19 Step 5. On H200s with 8 concurrent requests, p90 first sound was 2.697 and 2.663 s with the talker beside one thinker half, against 1.463 and 1.415 s on its own GPU. The means are 2.68 and 1.44 s. Only the talker's placement changed. These are public results, not a local benchmark or a promise for another workload.

Step 2 follows `benchmarks/eval/benchmark_omni_streaming_ttft.py:148-205`. The client starts `time.perf_counter()` before streaming the POST. It assigns TTFT only when a delta contains nonempty `audio.data`; text-only deltas do not stop that timer. It measures total time after the stream finishes and requires at least one audio delta. The displayed 0.0, 1.4 and 3.2 seconds are constructed teaching times, not benchmark output.

Step 3 constructs two arrivals at 1.4 and 2.5 seconds around the 800 ms playback chunk from Learning SGLang Ch. 20 Step 1. Chunk 1 therefore plays until 2.2 seconds, leaving a 0.3-second gap. The current TTFT script counts audio chunks but does not store their individual arrival times or calculate stalls. This step shows the additional per-chunk timing arithmetic; it does not claim a shipped stall metric or a measured 0.3-second result.

Step 4 follows `sglang_omni/profiler/comm_trace.py:14-34`, `sglang_omni/comm/engine.py:327-393` and `sglang_omni/pipeline/stage/runtime.py:665-682`. `SGLANG_OMNI_COMM_TRACE=1` enables JSON log records. `ts_ns` comes from `time.time_ns`; an `elapsed_ms` field comes from a local `perf_counter_ns` interval around the named operation, such as enqueueing or reading, not the whole request. Request A, chunk 7 and object `obj-17` are constructed identifiers, not captured logs.

Step 5 reads `sglang_omni/relay/cuda_ipc.py:526-581`. `elapsed_ms` measures the host-side wait. When start and done CUDA events exist, `receiver_gpu_wait_copy_ms` reports their GPU-event interval and `host_minus_receiver_gpu_ms` subtracts it from the host wait. The 12, 5 and 7 ms values are constructed to teach the fields. The difference is not, by itself, proof of CPU compute time. Worker queue, worker block and resume fields are optional and appear only on the thread-synchronize path.

Step 6 follows the shipped A/B protocol in `benchmarks/eval/benchmark_omni_streaming_ttft.py:11-25,59-69,126-145,224-283,316-340`. Baseline uses `--no-enable-partial-start`; treatment uses `--enable-partial-start --partial-start-min-chunks 5`. Both labels use the same short and medium prompts, seed 1000, maximum 256 tokens and talker sampling settings. Each prompt gets two excluded warmups and five measured repeats, giving four warmups and ten kept runs per label. This step shows protocol only and claims no winning result.

Step 7 follows the aggregate calculations at `benchmarks/eval/benchmark_omni_streaming_ttft.py:245-296`: mean, minimum, maximum and population standard deviation. The five values 1.3, 1.4, 1.3, 1.4 and 2.6 seconds are constructed. They sum to 8.0 seconds, so the mean is 1.60 seconds; the population stdev is about 0.502. These values teach why per-run results and spread belong beside the mean. They are not partial-start measurements.

Step 8 combines the benchmark's per-run records and condition fields with the runbook's fixed conditions and completeness checks (`benchmarks/eval/benchmark_omni_streaming_ttft.py:354-365`; `docs/developer_reference/omni_offline_bench.md:310-379,435-443`). A comparison keeps the input, output lengths, hardware, configuration and warmup visible, checks completion and output quality, and reports spread as well as the mean. `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/sglang/sglang-omni/batch.md:221-235` gives the same boundary: performance numbers do not replace quality checks, and repeated requests within one run are not independent experiment repeats. The next chapter is `09-ar-stage-optimization`; this chapter does not claim that a cache, asynchronous decode or a CUDA graph already helps.

Code references in this chapter pin to `sglang-omni origin/main@3a31bd72e034db37ce5115a03dd4827b449683be`. Each public measurement keeps its original setup. The benchmark is read from source, not executed.

<details class="i18n-zh">
<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

第 1 步沿用 PR #1235 的数字，它们已经在 Learning SGLang 第 19 章第 5 步出现。H200 上同时 8 个请求时，talker 与 thinker 的一半同卡，首音 p90 两次为 2.697、2.663 s；talker 独占 GPU 时为 1.463、1.415 s。均值分别是 2.68 和 1.44 s，只有 talker 的位置不同。这些是公开结果，不是本机实测，也不保证其他负载得到同样结果。

第 2 步依据 `benchmarks/eval/benchmark_omni_streaming_ttft.py:148-205`。客户端在流式 POST 前调用 `time.perf_counter()` 开始计时。只有 delta 含有非空 `audio.data` 时才记录 TTFT，纯文字 delta 不会让 TTFT 停表。流结束后才记录总时间；若一直没有 audio delta，测试会报错。图上的 0.0、1.4、3.2 秒是构造教学时刻，不是 benchmark 输出。

第 3 步围绕 Learning SGLang 第 20 章第 1 步的 800 ms 播放 chunk，构造 1.4、2.5 秒两次到达。chunk 1 因此在 2.2 秒播完，留下 0.3 秒空档。当前 TTFT 脚本只统计 audio chunk 数，不保存逐块到达时刻，也不计算卡顿。本步展示需要补充的逐块计时算式，不声称已有卡顿指标，也不把 0.3 秒当成实测结果。

第 4 步依据 `sglang_omni/profiler/comm_trace.py:14-34`、`sglang_omni/comm/engine.py:327-393` 和 `sglang_omni/pipeline/stage/runtime.py:665-682`。`SGLANG_OMNI_COMM_TRACE=1` 打开 JSON 日志。`ts_ns` 来自 `time.time_ns`；`elapsed_ms` 用 `perf_counter_ns` 计算局部区间，例如 enqueue 或 read 的耗时，不是整个请求。请求 A、chunk 7 和对象 `obj-17` 都是构造标识符，不是抓取的日志。

第 5 步读取 `sglang_omni/relay/cuda_ipc.py:526-581`。`elapsed_ms` 测量 host 侧等待。存在 start、done CUDA event 时，`receiver_gpu_wait_copy_ms` 报告它们的 GPU event 区间，`host_minus_receiver_gpu_ms` 再从 host 等待中减去它。12、5、7 ms 是为讲字段构造的值。差额本身不能证明 CPU 计算时间。worker 队列、阻塞和恢复字段是可选项，只在线程同步路径出现。

第 6 步依据 `benchmarks/eval/benchmark_omni_streaming_ttft.py:11-25,59-69,126-145,224-283,316-340` 的 A/B 协议。baseline 使用 `--no-enable-partial-start`；treatment 使用 `--enable-partial-start --partial-start-min-chunks 5`。两个标签使用相同的 short、medium prompt、seed 1000、最多 256 token 和 talker 采样设置。每个 prompt 先剔除 2 次预热，再保留 5 次测量，所以每个标签有 4 次预热、10 次保留结果。本步只讲协议，不声称哪边更快。

第 7 步依据 `benchmarks/eval/benchmark_omni_streaming_ttft.py:245-296` 的聚合计算：均值、最小值、最大值和总体标准差。1.3、1.4、1.3、1.4、2.6 秒这五个值是构造的，总和为 8.0 秒，均值为 1.60 秒，总体标准差约 0.502。它们说明为什么每次结果和离散程度要与均值一起报告，不是 partial-start 的测量结果。

第 8 步结合 benchmark 的逐次记录、条件字段，以及 runbook 的固定条件和完整性检查（`benchmarks/eval/benchmark_omni_streaming_ttft.py:354-365`；`docs/developer_reference/omni_offline_bench.md:310-379,435-443`）。比较时要能核对输入、输出长度、硬件、配置和预热，检查请求完成与输出质量，并同时报告离散程度和均值。`Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/sglang/sglang-omni/batch.md:221-235` 给出同样边界：性能数字不能替代质量检查，同一次运行里的许多请求也不是独立的实验重复。下一章是 `09-ar-stage-optimization`；本章不声称 cache、异步 decode 或 CUDA Graph 已经带来收益。

本章代码出处固定到 `sglang-omni origin/main@3a31bd72e034db37ce5115a03dd4827b449683be`。公开测量保留各自原来的实验条件。本章只读 benchmark 源码，没有运行它。
