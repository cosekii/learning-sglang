<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

This chapter follows `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/sys-design/readme-1.md` and `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/sglang/latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md`. Chapter 6 established that slime waits for `actor_model.async_update_weights()` before the next rollout; this chapter opens that update box.

Step 1 uses the debug note at `latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md:556-558,980-1007`. The nominal size is `1,000,000,000 weight values × 2 bytes = 2 GB` in decimal units. The 7.047-second full update and the separate `q_proj` tensor example's 0.001-second `ModelRunner` time and 0.033-second outer call are one author's distributed-update measurements, not a controlled comparison of transport methods. `q_proj` names the tensor in this debug example. The separate 0.6 ms figure derives 2 GB at the shared HBM bandwidth of 3.35 TB/s and is only a yardstick, not a transport prediction.

Step 2 maps the three interfaces at `sys-design/readme-1.md:400-409`, then opens the disk route. The card saves `v1` as a checkpoint, reads it, and loads each worker's own part. In the source implementation, `update_weights_from_disk` reads the checkpoint and calls the model's weight loader; those API names stay off the slide. Storage speed and loader behavior determine its wait; the sources provide no disk timing. The pinned implementation is `sgl-project/sglang@392e441ad17c78b68638f2d959fcf592d19b4834`, `python/sglang/srt/model_executor/model_runner.py:641-694`.

Step 3 follows `latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md:232-262,399-412,931-950`. The update group contains one training rank plus the inference TP ranks, so `world_size = 1 + tp_size`. For each weight tensor, inference allocates a destination, broadcasts the full values from rank 0, keeps its own shard and calls `load_weights` (`sys-design/readme-1.md:407`). Group-rank count is not a count of top-level processes. The pinned `ModelRunner` receive path is `model_runner.py:696-772` at the same SGLang revision.

Step 4 follows `sys-design/readme-1.md:51-60,132-204`. `full_tensor()` assembles the parameter before serialization. `MultiprocessingSerializer.serialize` returns reconstruction handles rather than the tensor payload, and `gather_object` gathers those handles at inference TP rank 0. Each SGLang TP rank deserializes its entry, rebuilds the full tensor and lets `load_weights` select its local shard. The fixed sender is `volcengine/verl@0508af25b66e839772fba8e79d97896bf0d843d3`, `verl/workers/sharding_manager/fsdp_sglang.py:160-188`; the receiver is `model_runner.py:774-792,1464-1479` at the pinned SGLang revision. The `[256,1024]` training shards and `[1024,1024]` full tensor follow line 51; line 53 accidentally reverses the two shard labels and is not reproduced.

Step 5 follows `sys-design/readme-1.md:254-272,280-296,329-395`. Slime offloads training weights and optimizer state after training, then restores only one parameter bucket at a time. Within a bucket it broadcasts across PP and EP where needed, gathers TP partitions, removes padding and converts to Hugging Face layout before sending handles. `ray.get(ref)` completes before the converted tensors are cleared. The figure shows the residency boundary but no bucket size or memory saving because the source supplies neither.

Step 6 combines the replacement boundaries at `sys-design/readme-1.md:268-287`, `verl/workers/sharding_manager/fsdp_sglang.py:98-135,164-188` and `latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md:451-458,1011-1058`. Generation must not observe a parameter-by-parameter transition, source storage stays alive until the awaited update finishes, and old KV state cannot be reused. Slime resets prefix caches before buckets; verl passes `flush_cache=True` with the last parameter. On failure, discard the partially updated model. The historical manager's lock, request drain and sleeps are shown as a race-condition workaround, not proof of crash-safe transactional replacement.

Step 7 uses `latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md:980-1007,1023-1030,1061-1093`. The outer example reports 0.033 seconds while `ModelRunner` reports 0.001; three 10 ms sleeps account for 0.030 seconds, with the remaining 0.002 affected by rounding. Removing those sleeps reduced the author's full 1B update from 7.047 to 2.202 seconds. The source loops over `state_dict` and calls `update_parameter_from_distributed` once per tensor, so the 30 ms block repeats about $4.845/0.030=161.5$ times, approximately 160 calls. The first communication remains slow after sleep removal, so the note adds a barrier immediately after process-group initialization to warm up before later measurements. Its local results are then approximately 0.5 seconds for 1B and 0.6 seconds for 8B; no exact warm-up duration or general speedup ratio is supplied. The recommended call is `dist.barrier(device_ids=[0], group=pg)`: omitting `device_ids` caused a device-error hang in CI but not locally. These are successive debug results, not a universal speedup. The note's timer uses `torch.cuda.synchronize()` around GPU work at lines 584-605.

Step 8 stops at the next chapter's problem. `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/fsdp/readme.md:16` distinguishes full per-GPU replicas of weights, gradients and optimizer state from sharded copies. This chapter names those three kinds of saved tables, excludes activations and temporary allocations from this step, and asks which slice each GPU can keep. It does not explain FSDP2's sharding or gather mechanics. The handoff follows `docs/plan-ml-sys.md:27-28`.

<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

本章依据 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/sys-design/readme-1.md` 和 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/sglang/latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md`。第 6 章已经说明 slime 会等 `actor_model.async_update_weights()` 完成再开始下一轮，本章打开这个更新框。

第 1 步使用 `latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md:556-558,980-1007` 的调试记录。权重大小按 `1,000,000,000 个权重数值 × 2 字节 = 2 GB` 计算，采用十进制单位。完整更新 7.047 秒，以及另一个 `q_proj` 张量示例的 `ModelRunner` 0.001 秒和外部调用 0.033 秒，都是一位作者对 distributed 更新的测量，不是传输方式的对照实验。`q_proj` 是这个调试示例里的张量名。另算的 0.6 ms 来自 2 GB 与共享 HBM 带宽 3.35 TB/s，只作对照，不是传输预测。

第 2 步依据 `sys-design/readme-1.md:400-409` 画出三个接口的地图，再展开磁盘路线。磁盘卡先把 v1 保存为 checkpoint，读取后让每个 worker 装入自己那份。源码实现通过 `update_weights_from_disk` 读取 checkpoint，再调用模型的权重加载器；这些 API 名不画上舞台。等待取决于存储和加载过程，来源没有给出磁盘计时。固定实现为 `sgl-project/sglang@392e441ad17c78b68638f2d959fcf592d19b4834` 的 `python/sglang/srt/model_executor/model_runner.py:641-694`。

第 3 步依据 `latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md:232-262,399-412,931-950`。更新组包含 1 个训练 rank 和推理端的 TP rank，所以 `world_size = 1 + tp_size`。每个权重张量都先由推理端分配接收空间，从 rank 0 广播完整数值，留下自己的分片，再调用 `load_weights`（`sys-design/readme-1.md:407`）。组内 rank 数不等于顶层进程数。固定 `ModelRunner` 接收路径为同一 SGLang 版本的 `model_runner.py:696-772`。

第 4 步依据 `sys-design/readme-1.md:51-60,132-204`。`full_tensor()` 先拼好完整参数，再做序列化。`MultiprocessingSerializer.serialize` 返回的是重建句柄，不是张量数据本体；`gather_object` 再把这些句柄收拢到推理 TP rank 0。每个 SGLang TP rank 反序列化自己的那一项、重建完整张量，再由 `load_weights` 选出本地分片。发送端固定为 `volcengine/verl@0508af25b66e839772fba8e79d97896bf0d843d3` 的 `verl/workers/sharding_manager/fsdp_sglang.py:160-188`，接收端为固定 SGLang 版本的 `model_runner.py:774-792,1464-1479`。训练分片 `[256,1024]` 和完整张量 `[1024,1024]` 按第 51 行绘制；第 53 行误把两种分片标签写反，本章不沿用。

第 5 步依据 `sys-design/readme-1.md:254-272,280-296,329-395`。slime 在训练后 offload 训练权重和优化器状态，更新时每次只恢复一个参数桶。桶内按需要做 PP、EP 广播，收集 TP 分片，去掉 padding 并转成 Hugging Face 布局，然后发送句柄。`ray.get(ref)` 完成后才清空转换结果。图中只画显存占用边界；来源没有给出每桶大小或节省量，本章不填数字。

第 6 步合并 `sys-design/readme-1.md:268-287`、`verl/workers/sharding_manager/fsdp_sglang.py:98-135,164-188` 和 `latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md:451-458,1011-1058` 的替换边界。生成不能读取逐参数变化的过程；源张量要等到更新调用完成后才释放；旧 KV 不能复用。slime 在分桶前重置 prefix cache，verl 在最后一个参数上传入 `flush_cache=True`。失败时，弃用部分更新的模型。历史管理器里的锁、请求排空和 sleep 只是竞态规避办法，不证明崩溃安全或事务式替换。

第 7 步依据 `latency-accelerate-for-weight-updates/readme.md:980-1007,1023-1030,1061-1093`。外部示例报告 0.033 秒，`ModelRunner` 只报告 0.001 秒；三次 10 ms sleep 占去 0.030 秒，剩余 0.002 秒受舍入影响。作者去掉这些 sleep 后，1B 模型的完整更新从 7.047 秒降到 2.202 秒。源码遍历 `state_dict` 并对每个张量调用 `update_parameter_from_distributed`，所以 30 ms 的等待块约重复 $4.845/0.030=161.5$ 次，即约 160 次调用。去掉 sleep 后首次通信仍然很慢，因此来源在通信组初始化后立即加 barrier 做 warm-up，再测后续更新。本地结果随后约为 1B 0.5 秒、8B 0.6 秒；来源没有给出精确的 warm-up 时长或普遍加速比。推荐调用是 `dist.barrier(device_ids=[0], group=pg)`：省略 `device_ids` 会在 CI 中因设备错误挂住，本地则没有。它们是连续调试结果，不是普遍加速比。第 584–605 行的计时在 GPU 工作前后调用 `torch.cuda.synchronize()`。

第 8 步停在下一章要解决的问题。`Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/fsdp/readme.md:16` 对照了每张 GPU 保存完整权重、梯度和优化器状态，与分片保存这些数据。本章只说出这三类保存的表，本步不展开激活和临时分配，并问每张 GPU 能留下哪一片，不提前讲 FSDP2 的切分或聚合机制。交接遵循 `docs/plan-ml-sys.md:27-28`。
