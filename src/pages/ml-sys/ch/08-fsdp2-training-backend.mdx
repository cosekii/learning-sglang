<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

This chapter follows `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/fsdp/readme.md` and `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/sys-design/readme-2-en.md`. Implementation claims use `THUDM/slime@55e41ffef7193fdd4c8bc14720233e446446b1d7`. The memory example is constructed arithmetic, not a training measurement.

Step 1 contrasts full replication with sharding at `fsdp/readme.md:16`. The constructed model has `4,000,000,000` values, with 2-byte weights and 2-byte gradients. Its optimizer convention comes from `readme-2-en.md:21-31`: FP32 master weights (the full-precision update copy), momentum (a running gradient average) and variance (a running squared-gradient average), or `3 × 4 = 12` bytes per value. Thus one full copy uses `4B × (2 + 2 + 12) = 64 GB`, 80% of the shared 80 GB H100 capacity. Eight copies use 512 GB. These numbers describe only the three saved table kinds, not activations or temporary allocations.

Step 2 uses the same source's FSDP alternative: each rank owns a shard of the parameters, gradients and optimizer state (`fsdp/readme.md:16-23`; `readme-2-en.md:43-47`). The equal eight-way split gives `64 ÷ 8 = 8 GB` per rank and 64 GB in aggregate. FSDP2's per-parameter DTensor representation is explained in the deep dive. The DP mesh determines the shard count: slime computes `dp_size = world_size // cp_size` and passes `dp_mesh` to `fully_shard` (`actor.py:158-186,94-98` at the pinned revision). Eight physical GPUs imply DP 8 only when CP is 1, as in this example.

Step 3 follows `fsdp/readme.md:18`: forward all-gathers the parameters for the unit being computed, then releases the full copy. The unit is the layer or parameter group materialized together (`readme-2-en.md:101-105`). The figure writes its temporary size as `U` because the sources provide no value. The `8 GB + U` expression is a residency boundary, not a measured peak; activations are still excluded.

Step 4 follows `fsdp/readme.md:19` and `readme-2-en.md:47-58`. Each rank trains on different records. Backward materializes parameters as needed, then reduce-scatters each layer's completed gradient so each rank retains its reduced shard for that layer. The label shows the whole-model gradient table: 8 GB ÷ 8 = 1 GB per rank. This is only the gradient table's shard, not the rank's 8 GB total training state or a layer's temporary size. The optimizer updates the owned weight shards after all layers finish. Slime calls ordinary `loss.backward()` and `optimizer.step()`; `fully_shard` handles the collectives (`actor.py:687-702,1000-1039` at the pinned revision).

Step 5 separates persistent shards from peak memory. Activations are intermediate training results and vary with batch size (`readme-2-en.md:31`). The source's formula with the largest fully materialized unit is explicitly for FSDP1 Stage 3 (`readme-2-en.md:93-107`), so the stage uses it only to identify memory categories. It does not present that formula as an exact FSDP2/slime measurement or assign values to `Umax`, activations or other temporary allocations.

Step 6 follows `fsdp/readme.md:134-140` and the executable updater at `update_weight_utils.py:45-74` in the pinned slime revision. The updater iterates model-state weights, redistributes each DTensor to `Replicate()` and gathers full tensors into size-bounded buckets. It waits for asynchronous results before handing a bucket to Ch. 7 Step 5's one-bucket-at-a-time transport. Ch. 7 Step 6 then waits for a complete version and rejects old KV. Gradients and optimizer state are not exported, and the whole training model is not materialized at once. The deep dive records the shipped 512 MiB flush threshold from `scripts/run-qwen3-4B-fsdp.sh:93-108`; it is a flushing threshold, not a hard memory cap. The closing question leads to Chapter 9's FP8 topic without claiming an FP8 mechanism or speedup here.

<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

本章依据 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/fsdp/readme.md` 和 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/sys-design/readme-2-en.md`。实现结论固定到 `THUDM/slime@55e41ffef7193fdd4c8bc14720233e446446b1d7`。显存示例是构造算式，不是训练实测。

第 1 步依据 `fsdp/readme.md:16` 对照完整复制与分片。构造模型有 `4,000,000,000` 个数值，权重和梯度都按 2 字节计算。优化器约定来自 `readme-2-en.md:21-31`：FP32 master 权重（用于更新的全精度副本）、momentum（梯度的滑动平均值）和 variance（梯度平方的滑动平均值），即每个数值 `3 × 4 = 12` 字节。因此一份完整训练表占 `4B × (2 + 2 + 12) = 64 GB`，是共享 H100 80 GB 容量的 80%；8 份占 512 GB。这些数字只算三类保存的表，不含激活和临时分配。

第 2 步使用同一来源的 FSDP 方案：每个 rank 拥有参数、梯度和优化器状态的一片（`fsdp/readme.md:16-23`；`readme-2-en.md:43-47`）。平均分成 8 份得到每个 rank `64 ÷ 8 = 8 GB`，合计仍为 64 GB。FSDP2 的逐参数 DTensor 表示放在深入讲解。分片数由 DP mesh 决定：slime 计算 `dp_size = world_size // cp_size`，并把 `dp_mesh` 传给 `fully_shard`（固定版本的 `actor.py:158-186,94-98`）。本例取 CP 1，所以 8 张物理 GPU 才对应 DP 8。

第 3 步依据 `fsdp/readme.md:18`：前向计算时 all-gather 当前 unit 的参数，算完释放完整副本。unit 是一起聚合的层或参数组（`readme-2-en.md:101-105`）。来源没有给出临时大小，所以图中记作 `U`。`8 GB + U` 只表示占用边界，不是实测峰值；激活仍不计入。

第 4 步依据 `fsdp/readme.md:19` 和 `readme-2-en.md:47-58`。每个 rank 使用不同训练样本。反向计算按需拼出参数，再对每层算好的梯度执行 reduce-scatter，使每个 rank 留下本层的汇总分片。标签写的是全模型梯度表：8 GB ÷ 8 = 每个 rank 1 GB。这里只算梯度表的分片，不是每个 rank 的 8 GB 训练状态总量，也不是本层临时大小。所有层算完后，优化器才更新拥有的权重分片。slime 调用普通的 `loss.backward()` 和 `optimizer.step()`，集合通信交给 `fully_shard`（固定版本的 `actor.py:687-702,1000-1039`）。

第 5 步区分长期分片与峰值显存。激活是训练中间结果，随 batch 大小变化（`readme-2-en.md:31`）。来源包含最大完整 unit 的公式明确针对 FSDP1 Stage 3（`readme-2-en.md:93-107`），所以图中只借它辨认显存类别，不把该公式当成 FSDP2/slime 的精确测量，也不给 `Umax`、激活或其他临时分配填值。

第 6 步依据 `fsdp/readme.md:134-140` 和固定 slime 版本的实际更新器 `update_weight_utils.py:45-74`。更新器遍历模型状态里的权重，把每个 DTensor `redistribute` 到 `Replicate()`，再把完整张量装进大小受限的桶。异步结果完成后，按第 7 章第 5 步每次交接一桶。第 7 章第 6 步再等待完整版本更新完并拒绝旧 KV。导出不包含梯度和优化器状态，也不会一次拼出整套训练模型。深入讲解记录自带脚本 `scripts/run-qwen3-4B-fsdp.sh:93-108` 的 512 MiB 冲刷阈值；它不是严格显存上限。结尾引向第 9 章的 FP8 主题，本步不宣称 FP8 的机制或加速效果。
