<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

Step 1 is a constructed timing example, not a hardware measurement. It illustrates the tradeoff in zero-to-sglang Part III Chapter 4 §2.1: splitting work can save compute time while communication launch, transfer and synchronization add waiting. The chapter does not claim that 8 GPUs are generally slower than 4.

Step 2 uses constructed tensors to show SUM all-reduce: corresponding positions are added and every participant receives the result. The operation follows `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/torch/torch-distributed/readme.md:510-526` and zero-to-sglang Part II Chapter 9 §3.1.

Step 3 follows the process-group example in `torch-distributed/readme.md:13-24,57-89,118-141`. Rank identifies a process and world size counts the participants. One process per GPU is the example's mapping, not a universal requirement.

Step 4 follows the PyTorch NCCL setup and SUM call in `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/torch/nccl/readme.md:41-53` and `torch-distributed/readme.md:160-175,286-292`. It separates the `torch.distributed` interface from the GPU communication backend without claiming a fixed algorithm choice.

第 2 步用构造张量说明 SUM all-reduce：对应位置相加，每个参与者都拿到结果。操作定义依据 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/torch/torch-distributed/readme.md:510-526` 和 zero-to-sglang 第 2 部分第 9 章 §3.1。

第 3 步沿用 `torch-distributed/readme.md:13-24,57-89,118-141` 的进程组例子。rank 标识进程，world size 是参与者总数；一进程一卡只是示例安排，不是通用要求。

第 4 步依据 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/torch/nccl/readme.md:41-53` 和 `torch-distributed/readme.md:160-175,286-292`，区分 `torch.distributed` 接口与 NCCL GPU 通信后端，不声称每次运行固定选择某种算法。
