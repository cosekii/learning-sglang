<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

This chapter follows `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/fp8/readme.md`. Constructed arithmetic and public measurements are labelled separately. Implementation references use `THUDM/slime@15fbd2ef82c765fe22f78e288db2bb79ebf582bd`, a reproducibility pin chosen for this chapter rather than a pin supplied by the article.

Step 1 uses the article's BF16 and FP8 storage discussion at `readme.md:40-54`. The 4B eligible-weight example is constructed from Chapter 8: `4B × 2 B = 8 GB` and `4B × 1 B = 4 GB`. The 2.39 ms and 1.19 ms figures divide those byte counts by the shared H100 bandwidth of 3.35 TB/s. They are ideal read lower bounds, not rollout latency. Scales and every other tensor are excluded.

Step 2 follows the scale and rounding mechanism at `readme.md:90-124` and the pinned converter at `tools/convert_hf_to_fp8.py:31-71,142-146`. The three-value example is constructed. E4M3 stores 176 for the scaled middle value, which restores to 3.142857. The 128 × 128 block accounting includes one 4-byte FP32 scale and does not generalize to the whole checkpoint.

Step 3 follows `examples/low_precision/README.md:23-27` and `tools/convert_hf_to_fp8.py:179-188` at the pinned slime revision. Eligible weights are converted offline with scales; activations are quantized dynamically for supported matrix multiplications. Embedding and `lm_head` stay in their original precision. The chapter does not claim an FP8 KV cache or a rollout latency measurement.

Step 4 follows `examples/low_precision/run-qwen3-4b-fp8.sh:117-143` and `examples/low_precision/README.md:21-23,51-54`. Supported `Linear` and `GroupLinear` matrix multiplications use FP8 in forward and backward; higher-precision regions remain, including attention softmax and gradient accumulation. Chapter 8's constructed 48 GB optimizer state is not halved because no cited source quantizes it to FP8.

<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

本章依据 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/fp8/readme.md`。构造算式与公开测量分别标明。实现引用固定到 `THUDM/slime@15fbd2ef82c765fe22f78e288db2bb79ebf582bd`，这是本章选择的可复现版本，不是文章给出的固定版本。

第 1 步依据 `readme.md:40-54` 的 BF16 与 FP8 存储讨论。4B 个可转换权重沿用第 8 章构造示例：`4B × 2 字节 = 8 GB`，`4B × 1 字节 = 4 GB`。2.39 ms 和 1.19 ms 用共享 H100 带宽 3.35 TB/s 计算，只是理想读取下限，不是 rollout 延迟。scale 和其他张量均不计入。

第 2 步依据 `readme.md:90-124` 的 scale 与舍入机制，以及固定版本 `tools/convert_hf_to_fp8.py:31-71,142-146`。三个数的例子是构造算例。E4M3 把缩放后的中间值存为 176，还原为 3.142857。128 × 128 分块的字节账只包含一个 4 字节 FP32 scale，不能外推为整个 checkpoint 的节省比例。

第 3 步依据固定 slime 版本的 `examples/low_precision/README.md:23-27` 和 `tools/convert_hf_to_fp8.py:179-188`。可转换权重提前离线量化并保存 scale，激活值在支持的矩阵乘法需要时动态量化。embedding 和 `lm_head` 保留原精度。本章不声称 FP8 KV cache，也不给出 rollout 延迟实测。

第 4 步依据 `examples/low_precision/run-qwen3-4b-fp8.sh:117-143` 和 `examples/low_precision/README.md:21-23,51-54`。支持的 `Linear` 和 `GroupLinear` 矩阵乘法在前向和反向使用 FP8，attention softmax 和梯度累加等区域保持高精度。引用来源没有把优化器状态量化为 FP8，所以第 8 章构造的 48 GB 优化器状态不能减半。
