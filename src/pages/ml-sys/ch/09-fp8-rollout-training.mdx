<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

This chapter follows `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/fp8/readme.md`. Constructed arithmetic and public measurements are labelled separately. Implementation references use `THUDM/slime@15fbd2ef82c765fe22f78e288db2bb79ebf582bd`, a reproducibility pin chosen for this chapter rather than a pin supplied by the article.

Step 1 uses the article's BF16 and FP8 storage discussion at `readme.md:40-54`. The 4B supported-weight example is constructed from Chapter 8: `4B × 2 B = 8 GB` and `4B × 1 B = 4 GB`. Supported means the documented FP8 path handles these weights. The 2.39 ms and 1.19 ms figures divide those byte counts by the shared H100 bandwidth of 3.35 TB/s. They are ideal read lower bounds, not rollout latency. Other tensors are excluded.

Step 2 follows the scale and rounding mechanism at `readme.md:90-124` and the pinned converter at `tools/convert_hf_to_fp8.py:31-71,142-146`. The three-value example is constructed. E4M3 stores 176 as the nearest representable value to 179.2, which restores to 3.142857. The 128 × 128 block accounting includes one 4-byte FP32 scale and does not generalize to the whole checkpoint. The shown converter and H-card training configuration use FP32 scales; `megatron_to_hf/processors/quantizer.py:96-108` shows that other update paths can use UE8M0 instead.

Step 3 follows `examples/low_precision/README.md:23-27` and `tools/convert_hf_to_fp8.py:179-188` at the pinned slime revision. The initial checkpoint's supported weights are converted offline with scales; later RL updates follow Step 5. Activations are quantized dynamically with scales for supported matrix multiplications. Embedding, the token-ID lookup table, and `lm_head`, the final-score layer, stay in their original precision. The chapter does not claim an FP8 KV cache or a rollout latency measurement.

Step 4 follows `examples/low_precision/run-qwen3-4b-fp8.sh:117-143` and `examples/low_precision/README.md:21-23,51-54`. Supported `Linear` and `GroupLinear` matrix multiplications use FP8 in forward and backward; higher-precision regions remain, including attention softmax and gradient accumulation. FP32 means 32-bit floating point. With `--fp8-param-gather`, stored weights can be FP8; without it, stored weights stay BF16 and are quantized for multiplication. Chapter 8's constructed 48 GB optimizer state is not halved because no cited source quantizes it to FP8.

Step 5 follows `examples/low_precision/README.md:42-64`. The common update handoff quantizes BF16 weights to FP8 for SGLang rollout. When stored training weights are FP8, Megatron first dequantizes them to BF16; without `--fp8-param-gather`, they are already BF16. The implementation accepts this BF16 handoff and produces rollout's own FP8 values and scales, rather than documenting a direct copy of training-side FP8. Checkpoints also use BF16 `torch_dist`. P counts supported weight values, and raw payload means weight bytes before scales and exclusions; no transfer latency is claimed.

Step 6 uses `InfiR2 arXiv:2509.22536v4`, §5.4 Table 6. The Qwen2.5-1.5B non-RL row gives per-pass totals of 2,030 versus 1,590 ms and peak memory of 68.1 versus 58.3 GB, producing 21.7% and 14.4% savings. The 440 ms difference belongs to one forward-plus-backward comparison, not an entire training run. Its UE8M0 recipe and unspecified efficiency-test GPU make this a public example of uneven gains, not a slime FP32-scale RL benchmark.

Step 7 follows `readme.md:204-261`. Real FP8 versus fake FP8 leaves a residual FP8 compute-path difference, including arithmetic and accumulation-path differences; it does not isolate only a kernel implementation. BF16 baseline versus fake FP8 isolates quantization round-trip loss. The experiment uses Qwen3-4B on one H800 during RL Steps 0 and 1. The article's Diff is a matrix-output difference with no stated norm or unit, so the chapter does not label it as a percentage, log probability, or KL value.

Step 8 follows `readme.md:275-369`. Published Qwen3 RL experiments report narrower `train_rollout_logprob_abs_diff` ranges and lower TIS-clipfrac under unified FP8. Only curves are published for the MoE comparisons, so no exact reduction is invented. The 0.08-to-0.04 gap bars are a constructed illustration on one scale, separate from the public direction. The curves do not prove zero difference or bitwise equality, meaning every stored bit is identical. The closing `2 B → 0.5 B = 75% fewer bytes` ratio is raw INT4 payload arithmetic before scales; `int4/readme.md:37-43,59-61` supplies the next chapter's QAT and STE boundary.

Further reading: [zero-to-sglang Part III Chapter 2: Quantization and Low-Precision Inference](https://zero-to-sglang.datawhale.cc/eng/part3/Chapter2_Quantization-and-Low-Precision-Inference.html).

<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

本章依据 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/fp8/readme.md`。构造算式与公开测量分别标明。实现引用固定到 `THUDM/slime@15fbd2ef82c765fe22f78e288db2bb79ebf582bd`，这是本章选择的可复现版本，不是文章给出的固定版本。

第 1 步依据 `readme.md:40-54` 的 BF16 与 FP8 存储讨论。4B 个支持的权重沿用第 8 章构造示例：`4B × 2 字节 = 8 GB`，`4B × 1 字节 = 4 GB`。“支持”指文档中的 FP8 路径能处理这些权重。2.39 ms 和 1.19 ms 用共享 H100 带宽 3.35 TB/s 计算，只是理想读取下限，不是 rollout 延迟。其他张量均不计入。

第 2 步依据 `readme.md:90-124` 的 scale 与舍入机制，以及固定版本 `tools/convert_hf_to_fp8.py:31-71,142-146`。三个数的例子是构造算例。E4M3 把 179.2 存为最接近的可表示值 176，还原为 3.142857。128 × 128 分块的字节账只包含一个 4 字节 FP32 scale，不能外推为整个 checkpoint 的节省比例。图中的转换器和 H 卡训练配置使用 FP32 scale；`megatron_to_hf/processors/quantizer.py:96-108` 表明其他更新路径可用 UE8M0。

第 3 步依据固定 slime 版本的 `examples/low_precision/README.md:23-27` 和 `tools/convert_hf_to_fp8.py:179-188`。初始 checkpoint 中可支持的权重提前离线量化并保存 scale，后续 RL 更新走第 5 步。激活值在支持的矩阵乘法需要时连同 scale 动态量化。embedding 是 token ID 查表层，`lm_head` 是最终打分层，两者保留原精度。本章不声称 FP8 KV cache，也不给出 rollout 延迟实测。

第 4 步依据 `examples/low_precision/run-qwen3-4b-fp8.sh:117-143` 和 `examples/low_precision/README.md:21-23,51-54`。支持的 `Linear` 和 `GroupLinear` 矩阵乘法在前向和反向使用 FP8，attention softmax 和梯度累加等区域保持高精度。FP32 是 32 位浮点数。开启 `--fp8-param-gather` 时存储权重可以是 FP8；否则存储权重仍是 BF16，在乘法前量化。引用来源没有把优化器状态量化为 FP8，所以第 8 章构造的 48 GB 优化器状态不能减半。

第 5 步依据 `examples/low_precision/README.md:42-64`。共同交接把 BF16 权重量化为 SGLang rollout 的 FP8。若训练端存储权重是 FP8，Megatron 先将其反量化为 BF16；不开 `--fp8-param-gather` 时，权重本来已经是 BF16。实现接收这份 BF16 交接并生成 rollout 自己的 FP8 数值和 scale，文档没有直接复制训练端 FP8 的路径。checkpoint 也使用 BF16 `torch_dist`。P 是支持权重的数值个数，原始负载指不含 scale 和排除项的权重字节；不据此声称传输加速。

第 6 步使用 `InfiR2 arXiv:2509.22536v4` §5.4 表 6。Qwen2.5-1.5B 非 RL 一行给出每趟前向加反向总计 2,030 对 1,590 ms、峰值显存 68.1 对 58.3 GB，得到 21.7% 和 14.4% 的收益。440 ms 是一趟前向加反向的对照差，不是整次训练省下的时间。其 UE8M0 方案和未注明的效率测试 GPU 表明，这只是收益不一致的公开例子，不是 slime FP32-scale RL 基准。

第 7 步依据 `readme.md:204-261`。真实 FP8 对模拟 FP8 留下 FP8 计算路径的剩余差异，包括运算和累加路径差异，并非只隔离 kernel 实现。BF16 基准对模拟 FP8 隔离量化往返损失。实验使用 Qwen3-4B、一张 H800，统计 RL 第 0、1 步。文章的 Diff 是矩阵输出差异，未给范数或单位，本章不把它称为百分比、log probability 或 KL。

第 8 步依据 `readme.md:275-369`。公开 Qwen3 RL 实验报告统一 FP8 后更窄的 `train_rollout_logprob_abs_diff` 范围和更低的 TIS-clipfrac。MoE 对照只公开曲线，因此不编造精确减少量。0.08 到 0.04 的差距柱是同一比例下的构造示意，与公开曲线方向分开。曲线没有证明差异为零或逐位相等；逐位相等指每个存储位都相同。结尾 `2 字节 → 0.5 字节 = 少 75%` 是 INT4 原始负载算式，另算 scale；`int4/readme.md:37-43,59-61` 给出下一章的 QAT 和 STE 边界。

延伸阅读：[zero-to-sglang 第 3 部分第 2 章：量化与低精度推理](https://zero-to-sglang.datawhale.cc/ch/part3/%E7%AC%AC2%E7%AB%A0_%E9%87%8F%E5%8C%96%E4%B8%8E%E4%BD%8E%E7%B2%BE%E5%BA%A6%E6%8E%A8%E7%90%86.html)。
