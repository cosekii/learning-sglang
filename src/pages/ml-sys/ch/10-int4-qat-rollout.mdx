<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

This chapter follows `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/int4/readme.md`. Implementation references use `THUDM/slime@58525eb986c66a271aa31077e17b8afebe704b4f`, `NVIDIA/Megatron-LM@3714d81d418c9f1bca4594fc35f9e8289f652862`, and `sgl-project/sglang@dce8b0606c06d3a191a24c7b8cbe8e238ab316c9`. Constructed arithmetic and article-reported results are labelled separately.

Step 1 uses the article's raw INT4 storage discussion at `readme.md:101-114`. The 32-value group is constructed: `32 × 2 B = 64 B` in BF16 and `32 × 0.5 B = 16 B` as raw INT4, saving 75% before scales, exclusions and runtime memory. Fewer numeric levels introduce rounding; this step makes no latency or whole-checkpoint claim.

Step 2 follows the fake-quantization mechanism at `readme.md:53-61` and the pinned `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:71-110`. The three displayed values are constructed from one 32-value group. The implementation computes an FP32 scale as `max_abs ÷ 7`, clamps it to at least `1e-5`, rounds and clamps codes to `[-7,7]`, then restores and casts to the input dtype. The example is nonzero, so it does not exercise the scale floor.

Step 3 follows `readme.md:37-49,63-78`, `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:124-150`, and the recipe's environment at `scripts/low_precision/run-kimi-k2-Thinking-int4.sh:158-170`. QAT means quantization-aware training. The hook applies fake quantize–dequantize to selected `TEGroupedLinear` weights with group size 32. BF16 storage and computation remain; the forward value keeps the rounding error. The article's asymmetric comparisons support pairing fake INT4 training with real INT4 rollout, not a universal BF16-equivalence claim.

Step 4 follows the exact backward implementation at `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:112-122`: `return grad_output, None`. STE approximates the whole round trip as identity for gradients and does not remove forward error. Megatron's `arguments.py:751-765` sets model parameters to BF16 under this recipe, while `arguments.py:3475-3484` defaults optimizer main parameters to FP32. The chapter therefore does not repeat the article's “BF16 master weights” label for both stores.

Step 5 follows `slime/backends/megatron_utils/megatron_to_hf/processors/quantizer_compressed_tensors.py:88-159,162-191` and `update_weight_from_distributed.py:93-154`. Real export runs during every RL update. It uses the same `max_abs ÷ 7` scale rule but literally clamps codes to `[-8,7]`, unlike fake QAT's `[-7,7]`. Scales return to the source weight's BF16 dtype. Kimi-K2-Thinking's public `config.json:79-110` quantizes selected expert weights and excludes `lm_head`, self-attention, shared experts and dense MLP projections.

Further reading: [zero-to-sglang Part III Chapter 2: Quantization and Low-Precision Inference](https://zero-to-sglang.datawhale.cc/eng/part3/Chapter2_Quantization-and-Low-Precision-Inference.html).

<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

本章依据 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/int4/readme.md`。实现引用固定到 `THUDM/slime@58525eb986c66a271aa31077e17b8afebe704b4f`、`NVIDIA/Megatron-LM@3714d81d418c9f1bca4594fc35f9e8289f652862` 和 `sgl-project/sglang@dce8b0606c06d3a191a24c7b8cbe8e238ab316c9`。构造算式与文章报告的结果分别标明。

第 1 步依据 `readme.md:101-114` 的 INT4 原始存储讨论。32 个权重是构造算例：`32 × 2 字节 = 64 字节`，原始 INT4 为 `32 × 0.5 字节 = 16 字节`，未计 scale、排除项和运行显存时少 75%。更少的数值等级带来舍入；本步不声称延迟或完整 checkpoint 的收益。

第 2 步依据 `readme.md:53-61` 的模拟量化机制，以及固定版本 `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:71-110`。三个展示值来自构造的 32 权重组。实现用 FP32 计算 `最大绝对值 ÷ 7` 的 scale，把下限设为 `1e-5`，编码舍入并截断到 `[-7,7]`，再还原并转成输入类型。本例非零，不触发 scale 下限。

第 3 步依据 `readme.md:37-49,63-78`、`docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:124-150` 和 `scripts/low_precision/run-kimi-k2-Thinking-int4.sh:158-170` 的环境配置。QAT 是量化感知训练。钩子以 32 为组大小，对选定的 `TEGroupedLinear` 权重做模拟量化再还原。保存和计算仍是 BF16，前向保留舍入误差。文章的非对称对照支持训练 fake INT4 与 rollout real INT4 配对，不能推出任意配置都等同 BF16。

第 4 步依据 `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:112-122` 的反向实现：`return grad_output, None`。STE 在梯度计算中把整段往返近似为恒等映射，不会消除前向误差。Megatron 的 `arguments.py:751-765` 在这个配置下把模型参数设为 BF16，`arguments.py:3475-3484` 则把优化器主参数默认设为 FP32。因此本章不把两种存储都称为“BF16 master weights”。

第 5 步依据 `slime/backends/megatron_utils/megatron_to_hf/processors/quantizer_compressed_tensors.py:88-159,162-191` 和 `update_weight_from_distributed.py:93-154`。真实导出在每轮 RL 更新中执行。scale 仍按 `最大绝对值 ÷ 7` 计算，但代码的字面截断范围是 `[-8,7]`，与 fake QAT 的 `[-7,7]` 不同。scale 转回源权重的 BF16 类型。Kimi-K2-Thinking 的公开 `config.json:79-110` 只量化选定的专家权重，并排除 `lm_head`、self-attention、shared experts 和 dense MLP projections。
