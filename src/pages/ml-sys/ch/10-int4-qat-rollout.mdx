<summary>Where this chapter's claims come from</summary>

This chapter follows `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/int4/readme.md`. Implementation references use `THUDM/slime@58525eb986c66a271aa31077e17b8afebe704b4f`, `NVIDIA/Megatron-LM@3714d81d418c9f1bca4594fc35f9e8289f652862`, and `sgl-project/sglang@dce8b0606c06d3a191a24c7b8cbe8e238ab316c9`. Constructed arithmetic and article-reported results are labelled separately.

Step 1 uses the article's raw INT4 storage discussion at `readme.md:101-114`. The 32-value group is constructed: `32 × 2 B = 64 B` in BF16 and `32 × 0.5 B = 16 B` as raw INT4, saving 75% before scales, exclusions and runtime memory. Fewer numeric levels introduce rounding; this step makes no latency or whole-checkpoint claim.

Step 2 follows the fake-quantization mechanism at `readme.md:53-61` and the pinned `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:71-110`. The three displayed values come from a constructed 32-value group whose other members also have magnitude at most 0.70. This rule makes 7 its positive code limit; it is not a universal INT4 definition. The implementation computes an FP32 scale as `max_abs ÷ 7`, clamps it to at least `1e-5`, rounds and clamps codes to `[-7,7]`, then restores and casts to the input dtype. Display decimals are rounded for readability rather than presented as bitwise BF16 results.

Step 3 follows `readme.md:37-49,63-78`, `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:124-150`, and the recipe's environment at `scripts/low_precision/run-kimi-k2-Thinking-int4.sh:158-170`. QAT means quantization-aware training. The hook applies fake quantize–dequantize to selected `TEGroupedLinear` weights with group size 32. BF16 storage and computation remain; the forward value keeps the rounding error. The article's asymmetric comparisons support pairing fake INT4 training with real INT4 rollout, not a universal BF16-equivalence claim.

Step 4 follows the exact backward implementation at `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:112-122`: `return grad_output, None`. STE approximates the whole round trip as identity for gradients and does not remove forward error. Slime's `slime/backends/megatron_utils/arguments.py:11-16` selects BF16, then Megatron's `arguments.py:751-765` sets model parameters to BF16. `arguments.py:3475-3484` defaults optimizer main parameters to FP32. Chapter 8's FP32 optimizer copy therefore remains distinct from the BF16 forward weights.

Step 5 follows `slime/backends/megatron_utils/megatron_to_hf/processors/quantizer_compressed_tensors.py:88-159,162-191` and `update_weight/hf_weight_iterator_direct.py:24-41`. Real export runs during every RL update. It uses the same `max_abs ÷ 7` scale rule but literally clamps codes to `[-8,7]`, unlike fake QAT's `[-7,7]`. Scales return to the source weight's BF16 dtype. The public checkpoint contains packed routed-expert weights and excludes the layers in `config.json:79-110`. Slime's converter instead checks `ignore`, `.weight`, rank and K-dimension without reading `targets`, so eligible non-expert tables can also enter an update. `megatron_to_hf/deepseekv3.py:6-10,106-108` shows embedding and router inputs.

Step 6 follows `quantizer_compressed_tensors.py:47-85`. Packing adds 8 to each signed code and places eight four-bit fields in one INT32. The complete 32-value group is constructed: 64 BF16 bytes become 16 packed bytes plus one 2-byte BF16 scale, totaling 18 bytes or 4.5 bits per weight. Its 71.875% saving differs from Step 1's raw-payload 75%; neither ratio describes the whole engine.

Step 7 follows `actor.py:126-133`, `update_weight_from_tensor.py:106-143`, and `docker/patch/latest/sglang.patch:732-823`. This recipe's `--colocate` selects `UpdateWeightFromTensor`. The update pauses generation, restores cached buffer shapes, transfers new packed tensors, repacks weights and permutes scales for Marlin, then continues. `restore_weights_before_loading` only calls `resize_` and clears the converted flag. It does not undo the old permutation or recover old weight values. Step 6's generic bit packing and this Marlin-specific ordering are different operations.

Step 8 follows SGLang's `fused_marlin_moe.py:77-92,140-143`, `dequant.h:161-183`, and `sgl-kernel/csrc/moe/marlin_moe_wna16/marlin_template.h:87-108,178-187,1290-1311,1635-1648` at the pinned revision. Marlin undoes the packing offset, multiplies signed codes by group scales to get BF16 weights, and uses `mma.sync...f32.bf16.bf16.f32`. FP32 belongs to that MMA accumulator. The atomic cross-slice path uses BF16 `scalar_t2`, and output intermediates use `hidden_states.dtype`, so the chapter does not call every reduction or output FP32. W4A16 does not set KV-cache precision or prove zero-cost dequantization. The article's performance comparisons at `readme.md:147-171` remain reported results.

Step 9 reads `metadata.total_size = 594205920512` from Kimi-K2-Thinking's pinned `model.safetensors.index.json` and divides decimal bytes by `1e9`. This is indexed tensor payload, not every physical checkpoint file. The article's 141 GB H200 capacity gives `8 × 141 = 1,128 GB`, `594.206 ÷ 8 = 74.276 GB` per GPU, and `594.206 ÷ 141 ≈ 4.21` for the one-card ratio. The recipe sets `--rollout-num-gpus-per-engine 8` at `run-kimi-k2-Thinking-int4.sh:119-139`. This proves only that indexed tensor bytes fit in aggregate, not that the full rollout fits or runs faster. Runtime memory and the article's reported one-node speed gain require separate measurements.

<summary>本章说法的出处</summary>

本章依据 `Awesome-ML-SYS-Tutorial/rlhf/slime/int4/readme.md`。实现引用固定到 `THUDM/slime@58525eb986c66a271aa31077e17b8afebe704b4f`、`NVIDIA/Megatron-LM@3714d81d418c9f1bca4594fc35f9e8289f652862` 和 `sgl-project/sglang@dce8b0606c06d3a191a24c7b8cbe8e238ab316c9`。构造算式与文章报告的结果分别标明。

第 1 步依据 `readme.md:101-114` 的 INT4 原始存储讨论。32 个权重是构造算例：`32 × 2 字节 = 64 字节`，原始 INT4 为 `32 × 0.5 字节 = 16 字节`，未计 scale、排除项和运行显存时少 75%。更少的数值等级带来舍入；本步不声称延迟或完整 checkpoint 的收益。

第 2 步依据 `readme.md:53-61` 的模拟量化机制，以及固定版本 `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:71-110`。三个展示值来自构造的 32 权重组，其余数值的绝对值也不超过 0.70。这条规则把 7 设为正编码上限，不是所有 INT4 格式的通用定义。实现用 FP32 计算 `最大绝对值 ÷ 7` 的 scale，把下限设为 `1e-5`，编码舍入并截断到 `[-7,7]`，再还原并转成输入类型。展示的小数为便于阅读而舍入，不代表 BF16 逐位结果。

第 3 步依据 `readme.md:37-49,63-78`、`docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:124-150` 和 `scripts/low_precision/run-kimi-k2-Thinking-int4.sh:158-170` 的环境配置。QAT 是量化感知训练。钩子以 32 为组大小，对选定的 `TEGroupedLinear` 权重做模拟量化再还原。保存和计算仍是 BF16，前向保留舍入误差。文章的非对称对照支持训练 fake INT4 与 rollout real INT4 配对，不能推出任意配置都等同 BF16。

第 4 步依据 `docker/patch/latest/megatron.patch:112-122` 的反向实现：`return grad_output, None`。STE 在梯度计算中把整段往返近似为恒等映射，不会消除前向误差。slime 的 `slime/backends/megatron_utils/arguments.py:11-16` 选择 BF16，Megatron 的 `arguments.py:751-765` 随后把模型参数设为 BF16，`arguments.py:3475-3484` 则把优化器主参数默认设为 FP32。第 8 章的 FP32 优化器副本与前向使用的 BF16 权重仍是两份不同存储。

第 5 步依据 `slime/backends/megatron_utils/megatron_to_hf/processors/quantizer_compressed_tensors.py:88-159,162-191` 和 `update_weight/hf_weight_iterator_direct.py:24-41`。真实导出在每轮 RL 更新中执行。scale 仍按 `最大绝对值 ÷ 7` 计算，但代码的字面截断范围是 `[-8,7]`，与 fake QAT 的 `[-7,7]` 不同。scale 转回源权重的 BF16 类型。公开 checkpoint 的打包权重位于 routed experts，并排除 `config.json:79-110` 列出的层。slime 转换器则检查 `ignore`、`.weight`、维度和 K 维大小，不读取 `targets`，所以符合条件的非专家权重表也可能进入更新。`megatron_to_hf/deepseekv3.py:6-10,106-108` 展示 embedding 和 router 输入。

第 6 步依据 `quantizer_compressed_tensors.py:47-85`。打包把每个有符号编码加 8，再把八个 4 位字段放进一个 INT32。完整的 32 权重组是构造算例：64 字节 BF16 变成 16 字节打包权重，加一个 2 字节 BF16 scale，总计 18 字节，即每个权重 4.5 位。71.875% 的节省与第 1 步原始负载的 75% 不同，两者都不代表整个引擎。

第 7 步依据 `actor.py:126-133`、`update_weight_from_tensor.py:106-143` 和 `docker/patch/latest/sglang.patch:732-823`。这个 recipe 的 `--colocate` 选择 `UpdateWeightFromTensor`。更新先暂停生成并恢复缓存的 buffer 形状，再传输新打包张量，为 Marlin 重排权重和 scale，最后继续生成。`restore_weights_before_loading` 只调用 `resize_` 并清除 converted 标记，不会撤销旧排列或找回旧权重数值。第 6 步的通用位打包和这里的 Marlin 专用排列是两个不同操作。

第 8 步依据固定 SGLang 版本的 `fused_marlin_moe.py:77-92,140-143`、`dequant.h:161-183` 和 `sgl-kernel/csrc/moe/marlin_moe_wna16/marlin_template.h:87-108,178-187,1290-1311,1635-1648`。Marlin 撤销打包偏移，用有符号编码乘组 scale 得到 BF16 权重，再执行 `mma.sync...f32.bf16.bf16.f32`。FP32 是这条 MMA 的内部累加器；跨 slice 的原子路径使用 BF16 `scalar_t2`，输出中间张量使用 `hidden_states.dtype`，所以本章不把所有归约和输出都称为 FP32。W4A16 不决定 KV cache 精度，也不能证明反量化零开销。`readme.md:147-171` 的性能对照仍是文章报告。

第 9 步读取 Kimi-K2-Thinking 固定版本 `model.safetensors.index.json` 的 `metadata.total_size = 594205920512`，再按十进制 `1e9` 换算 GB。这是索引记录的张量负载，不含所有实体 checkpoint 文件。文章的 141 GB H200 容量给出 `8 × 141 = 1,128 GB`，平均每张 GPU 为 `594.206 ÷ 8 = 74.276 GB`，单卡倍率为 `594.206 ÷ 141 ≈ 4.21`。`run-kimi-k2-Thinking-int4.sh:119-139` 设置 `--rollout-num-gpus-per-engine 8`。这只能证明索引张量字节小于八卡总容量，不能证明完整 rollout 能放下或更快。运行显存和文章报告的单机加速都需要另行测量。
